Digiiplex / Your account

Delete your account

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Request permanent deletion of your Digiiplex account and associated personal data. Digiiplex is operated by Digiiplex Entertainment Pvt Ltd.

Request deletion by email

You can use this option even if you have uninstalled Digiiplex or cannot sign in. You do not need to reinstall the app.

Email a deletion request →

Send to: support@digiiplex.com
Subject: Digiiplex account and data deletion request

Include your registered phone number with country code or registered email address so we can locate the account. Where possible, send the request from the registered email address. A reason for leaving is optional.

The button opens an email draft. Your request is sent only when you send the email. If no mail app opens, copy the address and subject into your preferred email service.

What happens next

  1. Account verification. Support may ask you to verify ownership using your registered contact details. If you have lost access to them, explain this in your email so support can help with verification.
  2. Review of paid access. Support will explain any active membership, rental, or renewal that needs attention before completion. You can initiate the request now and ask for help canceling renewal.
  3. Processing and confirmation. After verification and any required confirmation about paid access, support will process your request and provide the expected completion time. Support will explain any information that must be retained and notify you of completion.

Never email your password, one-time password, full card details, or payment PIN. Do not send identity documents unless support explains why a particular verification method is necessary and provides a suitable channel.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open Digiiplex and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account on Android mobile. On Android TV, look for Delete account in your profile or account settings.
  3. Read the deletion confirmation and any membership or rental warning. If prompted, turn off renewal and confirm the loss of remaining paid access.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion. If you cannot complete the process, use the email option above.

Information covered by the request

Your request covers your Digiiplex account and the personal data associated with it, including:

  • Account contact and authentication information, such as name, phone number, email address, and sign-in information.
  • Profiles, date of birth, profile preferences, and stored profile location information.
  • Associated watchlists, reactions, viewing history, playback progress, and other account-linked activity, subject to the limited retention exceptions below.
  • Active device sessions and account-linked notification information.

Once completed, account deletion is permanent and access to the deleted account and its profiles is lost. Removing one profile, signing out, clearing the cache, or uninstalling the app does not delete the account.

Records that may remain

Limited payment, purchase, tax, dispute, security, and deletion-request records may be retained where needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention, or legal claims. A limited internal record of the deleted account may remain linked to retained transactions. Retaining these records does not keep your account active.

These records are retained for the applicable obligation or purpose. Backup copies, where maintained, may remain until replaced or removed under the applicable backup process. Support can explain the retained categories, reasons, and applicable retention information for your request. Payment providers may retain their own transaction records under their separate obligations.

See the Privacy Policy's retention section for more information.

Memberships, rentals, and refunds

Deleting your account ends access to its profiles, remaining membership time, and rentals. Review these consequences before confirming. A deletion request is not automatically a refund request and does not cancel a separately managed payment-provider subscription.

For a Google Play subscription, manage cancellation through Google Play's subscription settings. For Digiiplex-managed renewal, use the available membership controls or ask support for help. Prepaid purchases have no automatic renewal. You do not need to wait for paid access to expire to contact support about deletion.

Refund requests and any rights required by applicable consumer law are handled separately. See Terms of Service — payments and cancellation.

Other privacy requests

To request deletion of particular data without deleting your whole account, explain the request to support@digiiplex.com. Support will explain what can be removed and whether it is necessary to retain information to keep the account or purchased access working.
