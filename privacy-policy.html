Digiiplex / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how Digiiplex Entertainment Pvt Ltd (“Digiiplex”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Digiiplex streaming app on Android phones, tablets, foldables, and Android TV.

Collection depends on your device, permissions, and the features you use. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@digiiplex.com.

Want to delete your account?

Visit Account Deletion for an email request option that does not require the app, along with in-app instructions and information about retained records.

1. Information we collect and why

Account and profile information
Phone number, authentication information, name, email address, date of birth, account and profile identifiers, profile type, and language and genre preferences. We use these to register and authenticate accounts, manage profiles, and provide profile-based features. If you choose Google or Facebook sign-in, we receive the information supplied through that sign-in flow.
Viewing and app activity
Search queries, content selections, watchlists, reactions where available, playback activity, and viewing progress. We use this information to find and deliver content, maintain your library and continue-watching list, synchronize supported features across devices, and understand content performance.
Device and technical information
Device and session identifiers, device model, operating system, app version, IP address, network information, and playback diagnostics. These support sign-in, device management, streaming, troubleshooting, service security, and fraud prevention.
Purchases and entitlements
Membership and rental selections, order and payment identifiers, amounts, currency, payment status, and entitlement history. These are used to verify purchases, provide paid access, and handle expiry, billing, disputes, refunds, and support. Payment providers process payment details entered into their checkout; Digiiplex receives transaction information needed to manage the purchase.
Notifications and support
A device messaging token is used for supported notifications. We also receive the information you choose to send to support and use it to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, one-time passwords, or full payment credentials by email.

2. Location and device permissions

Digiiplex collects location information for personalised advertising. On supported devices, granting location permission allows access to approximate or precise device location, depending on the permission granted. Latitude, longitude, and available city, state, or address information may be sent to update your profile's location information for this purpose.

The mobile app also uses IP-location services to estimate location when device location is unavailable. These requests disclose your IP address to the provider. Denying device location permission does not prevent IP-based approximate location lookup. Location integrations include ipapi.co and IPinfo; shared location features also use ip-api.com and ipify.org where enabled.

If you scan a TV sign-in QR code with your phone, the scanner requests camera access. The decoded sign-in token is sent to Digiiplex to authorize the TV session. Notification permission controls whether supported notifications appear on your device.

You can change location, camera, and notification permissions in Android settings. Some related features may then be unavailable. A permission request or this policy does not replace any separate consent required for a particular use of your information.

3. Advertising, diagnostics, and sharing

Ad-supported playback uses Google's Interactive Media Ads SDK. Depending on device settings and SDK configuration, it processes IP addresses, advertising or app identifiers, ad interactions, and technical diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and help detect fraud. Android provides controls to reset or delete the advertising ID where supported. These controls do not remove ads from ad-supported content.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports, installation identifiers, and technical information such as device, operating system, and app version. Digiiplex adds screen and playback-error context to help diagnose failures and improve reliability.

Information is processed by providers involved in the features you use:

  • Google and Firebase: supported sign-in, messaging, advertising, diagnostics, and Google Play purchases where offered. See Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Meta: Facebook sign-in where used. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Razorpay: payment checkout where offered. See Razorpay's Privacy Policy.
  • Location lookup providers: IP-based location estimation and related lookup requests described above.
  • Hosting, content delivery, and support providers: processing needed to operate APIs, stream media, maintain the service, and respond to requests.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law, valid legal requests, or to protect users and the service against fraud or abuse. Processing by service providers may involve systems outside India. External websites and sign-in or payment pages have their own privacy notices.

4. Local storage and security

The app stores session information, preferences, and cached media or images on your device to support sign-in and playback. Authentication tokens are stored using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local app data but does not, by itself, delete your server-side account or purchase records.

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to protecting information and restricting access. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

These legal pages do not use analytics scripts, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, or forms that collect account details. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to serve and secure the website. Email requests are handled through email services.

5. Retention and account deletion

We retain information for the purposes described in this policy, including operating your active account, providing purchased access, resolving support requests or disputes, preventing fraud, and meeting applicable record-keeping obligations. Retention depends on the record type and the reason it is needed.

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data through the app or by emailing support@digiiplex.com. See Account Deletion for the steps, ownership verification, and consequences for profiles, memberships, and rentals.

Account deletion removes account and profile information used to provide the service. Limited purchase, payment, tax, dispute, security, or deletion-request records may be retained where necessary for applicable legal obligations, fraud prevention, or establishing or defending legal claims. A limited internal record of the deleted account may remain linked to retained transactions; it is not an active account.

Retention is limited to the applicable obligation or purpose. Backup copies, where maintained, may remain until replaced or removed under the applicable backup process. Contact us for the categories of records retained in relation to your account, the reason, and the applicable retention information. Deletion of an account does not erase payment records held independently by a payment provider.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage available profile details and preferences in the app and device permissions in Android settings. You can also contact support to request access, correction, or deletion of your information or to raise a privacy concern. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a copy of your data, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

We may verify account ownership before acting on a request. Some information is necessary to provide account, playback, or paid-access features. Support will explain any relevant limits when handling your request.

7. Children and family profiles

Digiiplex uses date of birth to create a Kids profile for users under 18 and an adult profile for users aged 18 or over. The app does not currently enforce a minimum user age. A Kids profile label does not by itself establish parental consent or mean every title is appropriate for every child.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use and review content ratings. Where parental authorization is required by applicable law, it must be obtained. If you are concerned about a child's information or account, contact support so we can review the request and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the service or our practices change. The revised policy will show an updated date; additional notice or consent will be provided where required.
